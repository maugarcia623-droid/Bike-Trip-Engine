# Communication Style

- The user does not have a coding background. Always explain what happened, why it matters, and what comes next in **plain, everyday English** first.
- Avoid raw developer jargon (commit hashes, SPM targets, protocol names, actor isolation, schema migrations) unless needed — and if a technical term is unavoidable, translate it into a real-world analogy or rider-facing outcome (e.g., "saved your work to the cloud backup," "built the app's offline memory").
- Structure milestone updates around **what the app can do now** and **what you'll see next**, keeping any code or verification references brief and secondary.
